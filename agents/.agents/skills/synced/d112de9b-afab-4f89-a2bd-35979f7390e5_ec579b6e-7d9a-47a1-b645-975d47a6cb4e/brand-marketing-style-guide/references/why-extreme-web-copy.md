From large sports venues and healthcare systems to retail enterprises with thousands of locations, customers rely on Extreme to deliver resilient, high-performance networks at scale through a single, unified architecture.

**Simplifying secure, scalable, zero touch networking**

Extreme delivers secure, scalable, zero touch networking through an integrated, identity-driven architecture that simplifies security and operations while increasing speed, agility, and operational consistency. With Extreme Platform ONE Security, organizations can automate secure device onboarding, enforce consistent zero trust policies across wired, wireless, cloud, and remote environments, and contain threats through Fabric-enabled segmentation that limits lateral movement.

**The Challenge:** Networks must block lateral movement while auto-onboarding devices, enforcing consistent identity-driven policy across all points of access (campus, cloud, remote), and maintaining sub-second failover in mission-critical environments.

**Customer Proof:** [ADRZ Hospital](https://www.extremenetworks.com/resources/case-study/adrz-delivers-uninterrupted-care-for-eleven-years) operates with 11 years of uninterrupted service and zero downtime. **Why Extreme:** Extreme Platform ONE Security unifies Cloud NAC, Universal ZTNA, and fabric-enabled microsegmentation within a single identity-based architecture. By embedding zero trust directly into the network fabric and cloud management layers, organizations reduce complexity, eliminate integration gaps, and simplify operations. Identity-based policies follow users and devices across wired, wireless, cloud, and remote environments, while fabric automates hyper-segmentation to limit lateral movement and contain threats. Integrated visibility and automated enforcement accelerate zero trust deployments from weeks to hours.
